How It Works
Our Process

A straightforward path from your idea to a finished deliverable.

Step 01

Consult

Share your idea, challenge, or requirements. We'll discuss scope, materials, timeline, and budget to define the project clearly.

Step 02

Design

We create detailed 3D models in Fusion 360, iterate with your feedback, and finalize the design before any material is used.

Step 03

Prototype

A physical prototype is produced for you to test, evaluate, and approve. Revisions are quick and affordable at this stage.

Step 04

Deliver

Final parts, fixtures, or designs are produced and delivered to your specifications — on time and ready for use.
